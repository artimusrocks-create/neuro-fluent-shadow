Mission 01

Weekend replay

Поставь глагол в прошедшее время. Помни: find → found, win → won.

  1. On Saturday I Brawl Stars for two hours. play
  2. My team three games. win
  3. I a new brawler. get
  4. Then I a Harry Potter movie. watch
  5. Harry on a broom. fly
  6. We pizza for dinner. eat
  7. My dad a new car. buy
  8. It really fast! be
  9. I my homework on Saturday. not / do
  10. I it on Sunday. do

Неправильные: win → won, get → got, fly → flew, eat → ate, buy → bought, do → did, be → was / were.

Mission 02

Bug hunt

В каждой паре одно предложение с багом. Выбери правильное.

Возраст через am: I am 13. С игрой без with: play Fortnite. С людьми with: play with friends.

Mission 03

Callouts

Что ты крикнешь в войс-чат? Впиши слово.

  1. Your HP is almost zero: "I'm !"
  2. You need your friend to protect you: " me!"
  3. An enemy is on the left: "Enemy on the !"
  4. You are down: " me!"
  5. Your team won: ", well played!"
  6. You want to attack now: " them!"
  7. Danger behind your friend: "Watch !"
  8. You need more protection: "I need ."
