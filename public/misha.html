03 · Grammar patch

Bug fix

Это твой текст из анкеты. В нём три бага. Найдём и починим.

Hello my name Mickhail. I am 13 years old. I like playing with Fortnite and badminton. I live in Sochi.

Patch 1.0

  1. Hello, my name Misha.
  2. I thirteen years old.
  3. My friend fourteen.
  4. I like playing Fortnite.
  5. I play badminton my friends.
  6. My friend a dog and two cats.

Возраст по-английски через am / is: I am 13. «I have 13» звучит как «у меня есть 13». With ставим с людьми (with my friends), а с игрой не нужно: play Fortnite. «—» значит «ничего не ставим».

05 · Past simple

Replay mode

Рассказываем про прошлую катку. Обычные глаголы получают -ed, а неправильные живут своей жизнью.

  1. Last weekend I with my cousin. play
  2. We on a mountain. land
  3. I a medkit in a box. find
  4. My cousin a huge tower. build
  5. We a car behind us. hear
  6. We , but it was fun. not / win
  7. I to bed very late. go
  8. In the morning I so tired. be

Irregular loot

find → found · build → built · hear → heard · go → went · win → won · get → got · see → saw · be → was / were

06 · Squad talk

Callouts

Эти фразы реально кричат в голосовом чате. Сопоставь с переводом.

Enemy on the left!
I'm low!
I need shields.
Push them!
Watch out!
Revive me!
Cover me!
GG, well played!

Now say it

  1. Your HP is almost zero. You shout: "I'm !"
  2. You are down. You shout: " me!"
  3. You see an enemy behind your friend: " out!"
  4. You want to heal. You say: " me!"
